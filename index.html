Questions

Good to know.

Still curious? Call (980) 888-5780 — we're happy to talk through it.

Do I need to provide water or power?

No. Our mobile rig is fully self-contained — we bring our own water and power, so all we need is a place to park near your vehicle.

How long does a detail take?

An Essential Clean runs about 1–2 hours; a Signature Detail 3–4; an Elite Restoration with ceramic coating can be a full day. We'll give you a precise window when you book.

How do I get an exact price?

Pricing depends on vehicle size and condition. Send a few photos when you book and we'll confirm an exact quote — never a surprise at the end.

How do you protect the paint long-term?

Our Elite Restoration includes a 9H ceramic coating for years of gloss and hydrophobic protection. We'll recommend a maintenance plan to keep it looking its best.

What areas do you serve?

Charlotte plus Matthews, Monroe, Indian Trail, Waxhaw, Weddington, and Concord. Outside that radius? Reach out — we may still be able to help.

How do I pay?

We accept all major cards and digital payment after you've inspected and approved the finish.
